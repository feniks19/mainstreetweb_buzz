Guerrilla Marketing Ideas for Orange County Businesses

Creative, low-cost strategies to get noticed locally.

What is Guerrilla Marketing?

Guerrilla marketing is a low-cost, high-impact marketing strategy that uses creativity and unconventional tactics to reach your target audience.

Ideas for Orange County Small Businesses

  • Chalk Art: Create eye-catching chalk art on sidewalks near your business.
  • Flyer Distribution: Hand out flyers at local events, farmers markets, and community gatherings.
  • Pop-Up Events: Set up a temporary display in high-traffic areas.
  • Local Partnerships: Cross-promote with other local businesses.
  • Signage: Use creative signs and props to attract attention.

Why Guerrilla Marketing Works

It creates a memorable experience that people talk about. It's cost-effective and can generate significant word-of-mouth exposure.
